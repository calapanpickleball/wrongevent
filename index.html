Part 1A

Wrong Outfit Parade

Before tamaan ng hiya, magparada ka muna sa harap ng mga judge suot ang wrong outfit mo. Kapal ng mukha lang ang kailangan.

Bonus points kung magkatugma ang outfit at paddle e.g chef outfit + frying pan? Iconic. Bahala ka na sa combo.

Guidelines

  • Outfit must be safe to move and play in.
  • No offensive, discriminatory, or culturally disrespectful costumes.
  • Footwear is your choice; heels, slippers, barefoot — at your own risk.
  • Judged parade-style, one at a time.
  • Judges' decisions are final.

Part 1B

Wrong Paddle Challenge

Iwan mo muna yung Boomstik mo. Grab something na hindi dapat pang-pickleball — tara laban.

Bring Your Own Wrong Paddle

🍳Frying Pan
🩴Flip-Flop
🥄Big Spoon
🧹Broom
🪵Wooden Board
📕Book / Notebook

Guidelines

  • Bring your own wrong paddle — handheld and safe to swing.
  • Racket sports paddles or rackets are not allowed (tennis, badminton, squash, table tennis) — fairness lang.
  • No sharp edges, nails, glass, blades, or anything dangerous.
  • No offensive or discriminatory items as a paddle or prop.
  • Stick to your wrong paddle for the whole challenge — no switching.
  • Paddle breaks mid-game? That's on you.
  • Organizers have final say on what's allowed.

The Challenge — 4 Rounds

1
3 TRIES

Service Test

Kaya mo bang mag-serve? 3 tries lang.

1 in → advance. Palpak ang 3 → out.

2
FIRST TO 2 · PICK YOUR OPPONENT

Dink Battle

1 vs. 1 sa kitchen line, Bawal volley, Bawal labas sa kitchen.

First to 2, advance. Matalo, tapos.

3
FIRST TO 2 · PICK YOUR PARTNER

Doubles Battle

2 vs. 2, tara partner. Blaming your partner's paddle is encouraged.

First team to 2, advance.

4
FIRST TO 2 · FINALS

Singles Showdown

Last two standing, final laban na 'to.

Winner = Wrong Paddle Champion.
